OmniSync Hub — Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

OmniSync Hub ("the Application") respects your privacy and adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Data Accessed & Collected

  • Google Account Profile: Email address, display name, and profile avatar via Firebase Authentication solely to authenticate your workspace session.
  • Google Drive Metadata (Read-Only): When you explicitly authorize the drive.readonly scope, OmniSync Hub reads file names, MIME types, modification timestamps, and web view links to display your files in your dashboard.
  • GitHub Public & Authorized Data: Repository metadata, issues, and commit events for your configured GitHub handle.
  • Workspace Data: Tasks, notes, project portfolio entries, and connector configurations stored in your local device cache and your private, Zero-Trust isolated Firebase Firestore document tree (/users/{your_uid}/...).

2. How Data Is Stored & Protected

  • In-Memory OAuth Access Tokens: Google Workspace OAuth access tokens are held strictly in volatile memory and are never persisted to localStorage or external third-party servers.
  • Zero-Trust Cloud Security: Firestore security rules strictly enforce that only the authenticated account owner (request.auth.uid == userId) can read or write their workspace documents.
  • No Third-Party Selling or Advertising: Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train external foundation models.

3. Data Deletion & Revocation

You may delete any task, note, project, or bridge directly inside OmniSync Hub at any time, which immediately removes the record from both local storage and Cloud Firestore. You may also revoke Google OAuth access at any time via Google Account Permissions or contact the developer at grogusmom1989@gmail.com.
